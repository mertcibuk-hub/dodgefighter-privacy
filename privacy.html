Privacy Policy

DodgeFighter · Last updated: 26 October 2026

DodgeFighter is a game about miniature jets fighting inside a house. It does not ask you to create an account or register, and we do not collect personal information about you.

Stored on your device

Your game progress (missions completed, stars, selected jet and settings such as difficulty, sound and music) is kept only in your device's local storage. None of it is sent to our servers; it is removed when you delete the app.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads (to continue a mission or to start a mission with an extra life and double ammo) and occasional ads between missions. Ads are never shown during flight. AdMob may collect information such as your advertising identifier, device model and OS version, coarse location (country/region) and ad interactions in order to select and measure ads. We do not collect or see this data; it is processed directly by Google: Google Privacy & Terms.

On first launch the game asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency on iOS); in the EEA and UK Google's consent form is also shown. If you decline, the game works fully and ads are simply not personalised. On iOS you can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

The game is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from users under 13.

Data sales and changes

We do not sell or rent any data. If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new date.
