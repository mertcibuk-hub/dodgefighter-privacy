How to play

Drag with your left thumb to steer. On the right: fire (auto-fire can be turned on in Settings), the secondary weapon button (missiles, rockets or bombs — swap with ⇄), boost and brake. Every mission starts from your runway; finish the objectives, then fly home and land on the runway. Landing also repairs and rearms your jet.

FAQ

A mission is too hard

Open Settings and choose EASY: weaker enemies, more lives and more time. Flight assist helps you avoid walls.

How do I unlock new jets?

Talon, Vanta and Wisp unlock as you clear Blue campaign missions; Scarlet Hawk unlocks in the Red campaign. Pick your jet in the Hangar.

Where is the Red campaign?

Finish the Blue campaign's final mission and a BLUE / RED switch appears on the main menu.

I lost my progress

Game data is stored only on your device; deleting the app removes it.
